Entertainy — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

What Entertainy does. Entertainy is a personal video upload tool for its account owner. It sends the account owner's own videos to that owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, using TikTok's Content Posting API with the video.upload scope.

What data is used. To upload a draft, Entertainy uses only what TikTok requires for that upload: the authorized TikTok account's basic profile information (such as its open ID) and the video file the owner chooses to upload. No other TikTok data is requested.

What is not collected. Entertainy does not collect data from TikTok viewers, does not access other users' accounts, does not sell personal data, and does not use uploaded videos for advertising.

How long data is kept. Uploaded videos are handled for the purpose of sending them to the owner's TikTok inbox. Account authorization tokens are kept only while the owner keeps Entertainy connected, so uploads can continue.

Disconnecting. You may disconnect Entertainy from TikTok at any time through your TikTok account settings. Disconnecting stops further uploads.
