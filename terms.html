What Entertainy does. Entertainy is a personal video upload tool for its account owner. It sends the account owner's own videos to that owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, using TikTok's Content Posting API with the video.upload scope.

Drafts, not automatic publishing. Videos sent through Entertainy are drafts. The account owner reviews, edits, and posts each video themselves in the TikTok app. Entertainy does not publish videos automatically to the public TikTok feed.

Your content and responsibility. You are responsible for ensuring you own the rights to any video you upload, and that your videos comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Do not upload videos you do not have the right to use.

Accounts and access. Entertainy works only with the TikTok account that authorizes it. You may disconnect Entertainy from TikTok at any time through your TikTok account settings, which will stop further uploads.

Changes. These terms may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will change when they are updated.
